# Sahaf — EPUB Reader

A calm, fast EPUB e-book reader that runs in the browser and installs as an app.
Books are read only on your device: nothing is uploaded, and it works offline.

- EPUB 2 and EPUB 3, pictures, footnotes, tables, right-to-left and vertical text
- Page turning or scrolling, one or two pages side by side
- Remembers your page in every book; library with covers and progress
- Light, sepia, dark and black themes; text size, font, line spacing, margins
- Table of contents, bookmarks and full-text search
- 10 languages

Open: https://erenbaris838.github.io/sahaf/
